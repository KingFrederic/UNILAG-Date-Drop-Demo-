Frequently asked questions

Who can use UNILAG Date Drop? +
Currently enrolled University of Lagos students with a valid @unilag.edu.ng email. We verify every signup to keep the community safe and campus-focused. Alumni and staff are not eligible at this time.
How does the matching algorithm work? +
We use stable matching theory combined with machine learning. You answer ~50 questions about values, lifestyle, and preferences. The model finds optimal pairings where mutual compatibility is highest, and improves based on date feedback over time.
What if I get matched with someone I already know? +
Great! Use it as a low-pressure excuse to actually hang out. Many matches become close friends or study partners even when romance isn't the outcome. You can also tell us and we'll note it — no hard feelings.
Is my data safe? +
Absolutely. We're a Public Benefit Corporation legally required to prioritise your wellbeing over profits. Your questionnaire answers are encrypted, never sold, and used only for matching. Only your first name and UNILAG email are ever shared with a match.
Can I pause or delete my account? +
Yes — anytime. Simply don't opt in on a given week to skip it. To permanently delete your profile and all data, email us from your UNILAG address and we'll process it within 48 hours.
Can I set preferences (faculty, religion, etc.)? +
You can indicate preferences, and we weight them heavily. However, we occasionally override strict filters when the algorithm detects exceptional compatibility on the things that matter most — sometimes the best match is someone you'd never have swiped on.
