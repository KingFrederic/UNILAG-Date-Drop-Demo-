Frequently asked questions

Who can use UNILAG Date Drop?
Currently enrolled University of Lagos students with valid @unilag.edu.ng email addresses. We verify every signup to keep the community safe and campus-focused.
How does the matching algorithm work?
We use stable matching theory combined with machine learning. You answer ~50 questions about values, lifestyle, and preferences. Our model finds optimal pairings where mutual compatibility is highest, learning from feedback after each date.
What if I get matched with someone I know?
Great! Use it as an excuse to hang out. Many matches turn into study partners or friends. There's no pressure for romance—just genuine connection.
Is my data safe?
Absolutely. We're a Public Benefit Corporation legally required to prioritize your wellbeing over profits. Your data is encrypted, never sold, and used only for matching purposes.
Can I choose my preferences?
Yes! You can set preferences for age range, faculty, and other factors. However, we sometimes override strict filters if our algorithm detects exceptional compatibility—sometimes opposites attract!
